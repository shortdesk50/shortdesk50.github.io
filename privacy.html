Privacy Policy for Shorts Desk

Shorts Desk is used only by the owner of the YouTube channels it manages.

Data accessed: Through the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API, Shorts Desk accesses the owner's own channel information, videos, playlists, comments and analytics, only to upload and manage the owner's videos.

Storage: Access tokens and any data retrieved are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

YouTube: Shorts Desk uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).
